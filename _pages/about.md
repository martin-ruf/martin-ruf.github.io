  <section class="academic-research" aria-labelledby="recent-research-heading">
    <h2 id="recent-research-heading">Recent Research</h2>

    <article class="academic-research__item">
      <h3><a href="https://www.rsit-uni-tuebingen.de/app/download/12247723897/RSIT-WP-07-22.pdf?t=1705575546">The Effect of Taxes on CEO Performance</a></h3>
      <p>In response to higher taxes on compensation, CEOs are less likely to reach performance goals and spend more time working on boards outside their firm. Firm performance drops before eventually recovering as investment and compensation adjust.</p>
    </article>

    <article class="academic-research__item">
      <h3><a href="https://uni-tuebingen.de/en/fakultaeten/wirtschafts-und-sozialwissenschaftliche-fakultaet/faecher/fachbereich-wirtschaftswissenschaft/wirtschaftswissenschaft/lehrstuehle/betriebswirtschaftslehre/international-business-taxation/research/">Inheritance Tax Relief and Employment in Family Firms</a></h3>
      <p>German inheritance tax largely exempts business assets to protect jobs. Using data on inherited and sold family firms, this study finds no evidence that firm sales reduce employment; sold firms show stronger employment growth on average.</p>
    </article>

    <article class="academic-research__item">
      <h3><a href="https://uni-tuebingen.de/en/fakultaeten/wirtschafts-und-sozialwissenschaftliche-fakultaet/faecher/fachbereich-wirtschaftswissenschaft/wirtschaftswissenschaft/lehrstuehle/betriebswirtschaftslehre/international-business-taxation/research/">An Economic and Empirical Assessment of the Excess Profits Tax</a></h3>
      <p>The EU introduced a temporary excess profits tax for the energy sector in 2022. The paper examines how the practical tax base approximates theoretical excess profits and discusses the resulting economic and distributional implications.</p>
    </article>
